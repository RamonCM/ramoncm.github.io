I am a project manager and researcher working at the intersection of **digital government**, **smart cities** and **artificial intelligence**. I have more than ten years of professional experience, the last seven of them leading the design and implementation of digital solutions for Brazilian governments at the municipal, state and federal levels. My work covers the whole life cycle of a digital solution, from planning to delivery, including the training of public managers and operational teams.

I am currently a **PhD candidate in Systems and Computer Engineering at PESC/COPPE/UFRJ**, where I study how AI can support the design of institutions and public services, with a focus on human–AI cooperation, governance and technology assessment in the public sector. In parallel, I lead government-facing projects at **Lemobs**, a company based at the UFRJ Technology Park.

What I do
======
* **Project management for GovTech products.** At Lemobs I lead the development and adoption of digital products used by municipal governments (e.g. Maricá-RJ, Itabira-MG and Catanduva-SP) in solid waste, urban maintenance, works inspection and smart school feeding, from user diagnosis and solution design to deployment and continuous improvement.
* **AI applied to public services.** In the *BB Alimentação Escolar* project, a partnership with Banco do Brasil, I work on AI-based tools that support nutritionists in assessing municipal compliance with the National School Feeding Programme (PNAE).
* **Capacity building.** I design training materials and lead workshops for public managers and operational teams, connecting processes, technology and results.
* **Research.** My academic work explores collective intelligence, participatory methods and human–AI cooperation for cities and governments. See the [Research]({{ base_path }}/research/) page for details and publications.

Background
======
I trained as an **architect and urban planner** (EAU/UFF, 2014, with an exchange year at ETSA/University of Seville) and worked for four years as a BIM project manager before moving into digital government. I hold an **MSc in Informatics** (PPGI/UFRJ, 2022) with a dissertation on a collective intelligence model to support discussions about the city, recognised as the best master's dissertation at the Brazilian Symposium on Collaborative Systems (SBSC 2023). I have also taught as a substitute professor at the School of Fine Arts (EBA/UFRJ) and worked as an assistant researcher at Capgov (PESC/COPPE/UFRJ), facilitating participatory workshops with university staff.
